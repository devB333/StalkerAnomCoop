## What was built

The executor (`CoopServerHost.cpp` "S09-T02"): at Agreed the party enters
the **barrier** (inputs not applied, every action refused with reason 30,
in-flight durable work drained, 10 s at most); the `transition` record
phase 1 is committed with the source generation (the rollback point) and
the generation about to be taken; then, as the native `change_level` does
for one actor, `ClientSend`, both participant actors' ALife entities get the
changer's destination (graph vertex, level vertex, position — the second
0.8 m beside the first —, angles, torso), the arrival becomes each
player's entry point, `ClientSave`, and the destination generation is
taken through the ALife save itself (`request_publication(…, prepared)`;
`M_SAVE_GAME`'s `prepare_objects_for_save` would have written the live
positions back over the moved entities); the entities are restored and the
source level keeps ticking until the worker has published. **The
publication's pointer swap is the commit point**; phase 2 follows it, both
clients are told (CampaignEvent 13 state 8) and rejoin, and the serve loop
(`x_ray.cpp`) reloads the level in process: the sessions are detached from
the level, the destination generation is loaded the way a recovery loads
one (`CoopPersistence::load_generation`: same epoch, no replay), the level
and its identity registry stop and start (the registry re-binds the roster
from that generation's record and the saved actors are assigned to their
participants, exactly the S07-T04 path), the client stages, ticks and
identity check run again, and the host rebuilds its per-level state
(adapters, party tasks, the published set). Recovery logs a `transition`
record with its verdict and applies nothing: the generation it loads is
either the rollback point or the destination.

## Fixture and tool

`--travel c1,c2 --travel-variant 2`: round 0 of the travel fixture (p1
requests at `esc_level_changer_to_darkscape_1`, p2 accepts) and the agreed
travel is carried out; `--failpoint travel:before-take` and
`travel:after-commit` are the crash windows (`--restart-no-fixture`, the
server exits itself, `-ServerKill 0 -ServerRestartAfter 10`).
`tools/compare-coop-travel.py --variant 2` (checks `execution` and, with a
crash window, `recovery`).

## Limits found here

- The reload takes ~7 s on the server and the clients are back in the
  world ~20 s after the commit (reconnect, join, a 6-s load); during that
  time the party cannot play. S09-T03 owns the coordinated resume (both
  clients loaded before play resumes; today each plays as soon as it is
  in).
- The barrier drains for 10 s at most; work still pending then is
  logged and the take proceeds (not exercised: every run drained at once).
- A vehicle is not carried over (decision T02-6); the second actor stands
  0.8 m beside the first (T02-2).
- Only Cordon → Darkscape has been travelled (the fixture's first enabled
  changer); other passages and a return trip are untested.
