## What was built

**Who a mutant attacks (T02e-1).** A generic mutant's target is its enemy
manager's choice (`CMonsterEnemyManager`, per character, seeing every
visible actor), unchanged since S03-T04: with two players either can be
chosen. The fauna fixture spawns each class next to player 2 with player 1
120 m away and checks that the monster's enemy is player 2, never player 1,
and that player 2 takes a hit.

**The pseudogigant's threaten kick (T02e-1).** `CPseudoGigant::on_threaten_execute`
already took its enemy; the kick's camera shake, post-process and camera
nudge were added to the server's actor, which no one sees. On the campaign
server the kick is now presented to the kicked player's client
(`MonsterEffect` phase 4, value: the kick's strength); the client builds the
same effectors from the gigant replica's `threaten_effector()`. The hit, the
speed lock and the impulse stay on the server. Server log `monster-ability
… ability=threaten`.

**The psy dog's aura on clients (T02e-2).** The psy dog's aura (the
post-process when one of its phantoms is near the viewer) is the viewer's
own. On a co-op client the psy dog is a remote replica whose schedule does
not run (S10-T02c), so the client link now updates it through
`CPsyDog::replica_update_auras`, and the aura looks up the client's own
actor when it was not there at spawn. The psy dog's phantoms spawn on the
server when it has an enemy (either player) and take its enemy
(`EnemyMan.transfer_enemy`). **A crash fixed:** a phantom whose spawn fails
on a client is destroyed before its `net_Spawn` read its particle names,
and its constructor left them uninitialized, so the cleanup played a
garbage name (fauna-1: both clients FATAL); the names now start null and
the cleanup skips them. `CCustomMonster::net_Spawn` logs which part refused
a spawn (`S10-T02e custom-monster spawn refused by …`).

**Scanning.** `CScanningAbility` is included by the burer but instanced by
no monster in this engine; nothing to do (L12's scanning part closes by
source inspection: `scanning_ability.h` has no user).

**AI lighting (T02e-3, L13).** The server already lights both players and
one object in each player's view every frame (S03-T06, user decision
2026-10-19): the fixture's `lighting` line, taken over the fixture window,
reads `actor_tracks` and `view_picks` at exactly twice the frame count, and
each player's view list is its own (`view_count0` 8, `view_count1` 384 in
fauna-4).

**Fixture (`--fauna c1,c2`, profile `fauna_party`, server-driven;
`fauna_fixtures`).** Player 1 is moved about 120 m away (player 1 only,
L58); thirteen classes in turn (`boar_normal`, `dog_normal`, `flesh_normal`,
`pseudodog_normal`, `psy_dog`, `cat_normal_a`, `chimera_normal`,
`fracture_normal`, `gigant_normal`, `zombie_normal`, `snork_normal`,
`tushkano_normal`, `rat_normal`) are spawned 4 m in front of player 2, each
for up to 40 s, with player 2's health held at 1 and its wounds cleared;
a stage ends once its monster's enemy is player 2 and that monster hit
player 2 (player 2's last hitter, `coop_last_hitter`), the gigant's after
its threaten too, the psy dog's 8 s after its phantoms appeared; player 1
must never be its enemy. `compare-coop-fauna.py` checks the seven points in
its header; a stage whose monster fled from player 2 (30 m or more away at
its end, player 2 its enemy) counts as targeting it.

## Limits found here

- The Anomaly config variants (karlik, lurker, psysucker, bibliotekar,
  rotan, gigant jumper, borya) run their base class and stay **Untested**.
- A lone flesh, tushkano or rat flees from a player instead of attacking
  (its morale), as in single player; the fixture did not show them
  attacking in a pack.
- A psy dog phantom's spawn failed on both clients once (fauna-1, the reason
  not logged then; fauna-4's phantoms spawned cleanly); the client now logs
  the refusing part and survives it (bug list B11).
- AI lighting keeps S03-T06's rule (T02e-3): NPCs outside both players'
  views keep their last lighting; dynamic lights (flashlights, campfires)
  do not count.
