## What was built

**The rule (T02a-1).** The installed NPC schemes read the one global
`db.actor` (and `AC_ID`) for everything they decide about "the player":
`xr_logic.pick_section_from_condlist` and the `xr_conditions` evaluators
(82,000 reads a second at runtime, S10-T01 §2.3), `xr_meet` (greeting,
usability, the talk distance), `xr_danger`, `xr_combat_ignore` (10 `AC_ID`),
`xr_motivator`, `gameplay_disguise`. On the server, outside a participant
context, that global was the host's actor (participant 1): an NPC facing
player 2 greeted, measured and reacted to player 1. Every NPC scheme runs
from its script binder's update, so `CScriptBinder::shedule_Update` now
enters a `campaign::NpcEvaluationScope` for a living stalker or monster
(not an actor; server only), and the `db.actor` / `AC_ID` resolvers of
S03-T05 and S10-T04b answer, when no participant context is open, with
the alive participant actor nearest to that NPC (`campaign.npc_actor()`,
`campaign.npc_actor_id()`; `nearest_participant_actor`). A participant
context (a dialogue, an action, a task scope) keeps its own actor; the
engine's own AI (enemy memory, hits, danger) was per character already.
Counters `npc_scope_enters` / `npc_scope_reads` join the script-context
stats line.

**Fixture (`--npc c1,c2`, profile `npc_party`, server-driven;
`npc_fixtures`).** The alive stalker nearest player 2 is the subject
(player 1 is moved away along the line from the NPC when it is the nearer
one; player 1 only, L58). A Lua chunk is run twice, inside the NPC's
evaluation scope and outside it, returning `db.actor:id()`, `AC_ID` and
`xr_logic.pick_section_from_condlist(db.actor, npc, "{=dist_to_actor_le(D)}
near, far")` with D two metres more than the NPC's distance to player 2:
inside the scope all three answer for player 2 (`near`), outside for the
host's actor (`far` when player 1 is beyond D). `compare-coop-npc.py`
checks the five points in its header. No restart: nothing durable changes.

## Limits found here

- Script logic that runs outside any NPC's binder update and outside a
  participant context (smart terrains, squads, global managers) still reads
  the host's actor (L11 keeps that part).
- Two NPCs' logic can decide against different players in the same tick;
  a squad's shared decisions follow its members' own evaluations.
- L57 (NPC walking animation on clients) is not addressed here (T02a-2).

## Carried limits

  disguise inside an NPC's own update) follows the nearest participant.
