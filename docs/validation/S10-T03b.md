## What the scripts do (source)

- A squad's script target comes from `sim_squad_scripted:get_script_target`
  (`sim_squad_scripted.script:102-145`): a `scripted_target` (or a random or
  condlist target) of `"actor"` returns **0**, actor 0, player 1. The
  squad's `specific_update(0)` then makes the actor's server object its
  target (`se_actor:on_reach_target`, `se_actor.script:77-87`), and its
  online members walk to `squad_target:get_location()`
  (`xr_reach_task.script:251-269`), the actor's own position. Content that
  sends squads at the actor: Anomaly's bounty hit squads
  (`sim_squad_bounty.script:71,83`) and the measuring task
  (`tasks_measure.script:167`).
- Player 2's actor is an `se_actor` too (the class is the section's) and
  registers in the simulation objects (`se_actor:on_register`), so the same
  path works for its id. `se_actor:get_alife_task` reads `db.storage[0]`'s
  `loud_gvid`, which no installed script sets, so it is the actor's own
  vertex.
- **Offline combat** (`sim_offline_combat.script`) never reads the actor;
  nothing to adapt (T03b-3).
- The bounty trigger (`sim_squad_bounty.spawn_timer`) reads `db.actor`'s
  rank and reputation from a timer, outside any player's context, so player
  1's (T03b-2, a gameplay call left to the user).

## What was built

- `campaign.nearest_participant_id(x, y, z)` (`CampaignScriptContext.cpp`):
  the nearest alive participant actor's engine id, −1 when none.
- The compat layer's existing `get_script_target` wrap (S10-T02f) now also
  answers a non-companion squad's 0 with the nearest player to the squad
  (counter `squad_actor_targets`). Decision T03b-1.
- **Fixture** `--squad c1,c2` (profile `squad_party`, `CoopServerHost.cpp`
  `squad_fixtures`): player 1 moves to a smart terrain 300+ m from player 2
  (player 1 only, L58); a loner squad (`stalker_sim_squad_novice`, friendly)
  is created at a smart terrain 120–250 m from player 2 and farther from
  player 1, and sent at "the actor" as the bounty script does
  (`scripted_target = "actor"`, `rush_to_target`); its target must be player
  2's actor and its commander must come within 30 m of player 2 in 240 s;
  then player 1 is put 10 m from the commander and the target must become
  player 1's (0). `tools/compare-coop-squad.py` checks six points.

## Limits found here

- Bounty hunters are triggered by player 1's rank and reputation only
  (T03b-2, the user's call at G10).
- `se_actor:sim_available` uses the smart terrain nearest player 1
  (`smart_terrain.nearest_to_actor_smart`); it only matters in survival
  mode, where actors are generic squad targets (`target_precondition`).
- Not run across a restart: a squad's `scripted_target` is the installed
  squad state (`sim_squad_scripted` `STATE_Write`), saved like any other.
