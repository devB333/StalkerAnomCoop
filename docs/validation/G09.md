## Defects found and fixed during Stage 9

| Defect | Found by | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The serve loop paused the campaign clock twice around a reload (`running && !paused` assertion) | tv-7 | one pause per pass ([S09-T02](S09-T02.md)) |
| The reload could not find the destination generation's save: the engine's file index of the saves folder dated from the process start | tv-8 | the saves folder is re-indexed at the reload ([S09-T02](S09-T02.md)) |
| A client that crashed during the destination load and came back knew nothing of the hold and would have waited for the deadline | design review, S09-T04 | the `held` notice (state 10) ([S09-T04](S09-T04.md)) |
| A client back after the hold's deadline would have waited for a resume that had already happened | design review, S09-T04 | a right ready refused with no hold ends the wait ([S09-T04](S09-T04.md)) |
| The `held` notice was remembered as sent per player, so a client's restarted session was never told | tv-22 | a new session starts untold ([S09-T04](S09-T04.md)) |
| The `held` notice went out at the checkpoint acknowledgement, ~8 s before the client's world was live, and the client dropped it (events apply only to a live world) | tv-25 | the notice waits until the client is in the world ([S09-T04](S09-T04.md)) |

## Gameplay decisions to approve

All in [Stage 9 decisions](../stage9-decisions.md): **T01-1…T01-9** (the
prompt, 30-s expiry, no partner → no travel, disconnect aborts, combat
blocks, 20-s silence after DROP, one request at a time, the requester's
accept counts for nothing), **T02-1…T02-7** (nobody acts during the
execution, side-by-side arrival, the arrival as the respawn point, the
barrier rollback vs. the post-commit roll-forward, no undo after the
commit, vehicles not carried over, the same incarnation continues),
**T03-1…T03-2** (the hold, 60 s at most), **T04-1…T04-6** (who broke the
barrier, no rollback once the save is taken, a failed publication rolls back
and the game goes on, a recovered server holds nothing, the late or
restarted client's path, the 60-s and 10-s limits stand). The ones most
likely to need the user's word: **T01-3** (no travel while the partner is
away: the alternative is a one-player consent and changes D5/R10),
**T03-2** (60-s wait, then the present player goes on), **T02-6** (vehicles
not carried over), **T04-4** (a recovered server does not hold).
