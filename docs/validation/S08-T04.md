## The quest and the user's decisions

**Sidorovich, "Mobbing the Mob"** (`esc_m_trader_task_3`,
`configs/misc/task/tm_escape.ltx:275-297`): a repeatable fetch of 6–12
`bandit_patch` (`fetch_func = setup_supplies_fetch_task_lostzone_patch(…:6:12)`,
`tasks_fetch.script:459-487`), every dead bandit drops one
(`death_manager.script:223, 700-702`), one turn-in, `repeat_timeout =
43200` game seconds; its precondition is commented out (`:280`), so the
offer is not gated. Chosen over Wolf's mutant task (needs a random squad at a
smart, `tasks_assault.script:97-121`) and the Xenotech toolkit (one item:
nothing to split between two players).

User decisions (2026-10-24, plan.md R09, recorded under S08-T04): patches in
**either** player's inventory count and the turn-in takes them from both;
each player is **paid for the patches taken from his own inventory** (the
total equals single player); **both** players get the Loner goodwill and the
same side-faction changes; the possible bonus stash is **one** stash.

**Design change after q-2 (the anchor):** the first design made the
accepting player the holder. When player 2 held it, the task's variables
lived in player 2's actor's portable store, which the native save does not
hold (L32), so the task would not survive a restart. The party's task now
always lives on participant 1's actor (the anchor), whichever player asks;
the requester's own eligibility (alive, within reach of the giver) is
checked. q-5 and q-6 had player 2 accept.

**The local completion attempt** (round 3, every run from campaign-263 on):
the non-accepting client ran the installed turn-in
(`dialogs.npc_set_finished_task_complete`), `db.actor:give_money(10000)` and
`xr_effects.reward_random_money` locally (`quest-local-attempt … ran=1`).
Nothing reached the server: no completion until the real turn-in, and each
party actor's final money on the server equals what the quest paid on top of
its own (`local_no_effect`). The client's own money display can differ until
something sets it (limit below).

## Limits

- **One quest adapted.** Other tasks keep single-player semantics (the
  adapter acts only on `kPartyTasks`).
- **Dialogue UI:** players do not talk to Sidorovich through the talk
  window yet; the fixture sends the intents. Routing a client's talk window
  to these intents is S10-T04 (L01 dialogue).
- **Presentation:** the partner sees the party's news but not the native
  task in his PDA's task list; the bonus stash's map spot is made on the
  server and is not replicated, so neither player sees it (the stash itself
  exists); news sent while a player is away is not replayed.
- **Client money display:** a client's own scripts can still change its
  local money display (the server's money is authoritative and is not sent
  back to correct it).
