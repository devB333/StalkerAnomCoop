# S02-T04b — Native physics stepping and a falling body on the loaded level

Recorded 2026-10-18 UTC. **Completed.** On the loaded level
(S02-T05d), with no graphics device, the server ticks drive the native physics
world: a 100 kg wooden crate lifted one metre above its saved resting place
falls under the engine's gravity, hits the level floor and is put to sleep by
the physics world again within 1.2 s of simulation time, resting 1 mm from its
saved height. Every physics step was sampled on the server and on the normal
client running the same fixture on the same save (H06).

## What the native stepping is (source)

- **Frame to steps.** `CPHWorld` registers itself as a frame callback when the
  level creates it (`xrPhysics/PHWorld.cpp:185-200`, `Device().AddSeqFrame`).
  Each frame `OnFrame` calls `FrameStep(Device().fTimeDelta)`
  (`PHWorld.cpp:282-295`), which accumulates the frame time and runs
  `iFloor(frame_time / fixed_step)` native steps, carrying the remainder to
  the next frame (`PHWorld.cpp:506-560`). `fixed_step` is 0.01 s
  (`xrPhysics/Physics.cpp:47`; `ph_console::ph_step_time` copies it,
  `console_vars.cpp:13`), so the server's 20 ms tick runs two steps.
- **One step.** `Step` collides every active object, integrates the islands,
  updates the objects' data, then calls the world's step-time callback
  (`PHWorld.cpp:306-466`); the callback the level installs is
  `CLevel::PhisStepsCallback` (`xrGame/Level_network_start_client.cpp:156`),
  which does nothing in single player (`Level.cpp:1673-1678`).
- **Gravity.** `default_world_gravity` is 2 × 9.81 m/s² (`Physics.cpp:54`),
  set on the ODE world by `SetGravity` (`PHWorld.cpp:274-279`).
- **Per-step damping.** After each step `CPHElement::PhDataUpdate` divides the
  body's linear and angular velocity by `m_l_scale`/`m_w_scale`
  (`xrPhysics/PHElement.cpp:458-470`), both `1.01` by default
  (`Physics.cpp:31-32`), and adds an air-resistance force proportional to
  `k_l = 0.0002` times the speed squared (`PHElement.cpp:547-575`), negligible
  here. A free-falling body therefore follows `v' = v + g·h`, `y += v'·h`,
  `v = v' / 1.01` rather than pure `g·h·n`; the first fixture run found this.
- **Sleeping.** Each element tracks mean velocity and acceleration over
  `L2frames` steps (`xrPhysics/PHDisabling.cpp:100-160`); when every body of a
  shell is disabled, `CPHShell::PhDataUpdate` deactivates the shell
  (`PHShell.cpp:236-248` → `DisableObject` → `CPHObject::deactivate`,
  `PHObject.cpp:43-49`), which is what `CPhysicsShell::isEnabled` reports
  (`PHShell.h:236`). Waking is `CPHShell::Enable` → `CPHElement::Enable` →
  `dBodyEnable` plus `CPHObject::activate` (`PHShell.cpp:565-577`,
  `PHElement.cpp:572-577`, `PHObject.cpp:23-36`).
- **Moving a body.** `CPHShell::SetTransform` sets the ODE body position and
  rotation from a matrix, reinitialises the disabling state and clears the
  motion history (`PHShell.cpp:553-562`, `PHElement.cpp:237-260`).
- **Object follows body.** A physic object copies its shell's transform into
  its own `XFORM` every `UpdateCL` (`xrGame/PhysicObject.cpp:418-436`).
- **Contact damage.** `physic_destroyable_object` registers a collision-damage
  receiver for bones listed in the model's `collision_damage` user data
  (`xrGame/PHCollisionDamageReceiver.cpp:19-37`); a hard enough contact would
  send a hit and could destroy the box, which the fixture watches for.

Nothing above touches the renderer; the S02-T04a clock and the S02-T05d level
load already supply the frames and the world. No engine behaviour was changed.

## Fixture (patch 0012)

`xrGame/CampaignPhysicsCapture.{h,cpp}`, shared by the campaign server and the
normal client:

- **Object.** ID 10865, `physic_destroyable_object`,
  `dynamics\box\box_wood_01`, mass 100, one element with one geometry, box
  0.86 × 0.81 × 0.89 m, root bone `link` with material
  `objects\large_furniture`. It was chosen from the S02-T03d capture as the
  wooden crate farthest from any other collidable object (nearest shell or
  skeleton 20.7 m away; the actor 39 m away), so nothing but the floor can
  touch it. `-s02-physics-object <id>` overrides the ID. The fixture refuses an
  object without an active shell, mass or a static floor within 10 m.
- **Floor.** A static-only ray pick (`rqtStatic`, the level CFORM) from 0.1 m
  above the object's origin: triangle 1299382, material
  `materials\wooden_board`, 0.100 m below the origin.
- **Lift.** The object's transform with `y + 1.0`, applied with
  `SetTransform(…, mh_clear)`, zero linear and angular velocity, `Enable()`.
- **Sampling.** The world's step-time callback is replaced by a wrapper that
  first calls `CLevel::PhisStepsCallback` and then reads the body's transform
  (`GetGlobalTransformDynamic`), linear and angular velocity, `isEnabled`, and
  the contacts counted since the last step through an added object-contact
  callback (`add_ObjectContactCallback`, which keeps the shell's own
  callbacks). The first contact records both materials. Sampling stops 50 steps
  after the world deactivates the body, after 600 steps, or when the object is
  destroyed; the callbacks are then removed and the level's restored.
- **Pass rule.** The body touched something after falling at least half the
  drop; the first contact's other material is the floor's; the world
  deactivated the body and it is still asleep with speed < 0.05 m/s; it rests
  within 0.15 m of its saved height; the free-fall samples match the native
  recurrence above within 1 % (velocity) and 5 mm (position); nothing is
  destroyed or non-finite. The CSV rows are documented in the header.

**Server.** After `campaign-collision-passed`, `xrEngine/x_ray.cpp` begins the
fixture, resumes the clock, runs single ticks until the fixture reports done
(budget 400 ticks), pauses the clock and ends the fixture; stage
`campaign-physics-passed` (exit 14 on failure). `Run-Stage2Smoke.ps1` expects
the stage for GameState and copies the CSV to the run log as `server-physics.csv`.

**Client.** `Run-ClientCollisionCapture.ps1 -Run <run> -Capture physics`
(runtime from `python3 tools/prepare-stage2-smoke.py <run> S02-T04b`) starts
the rebuilt client with `-s02-physics-capture` and the same `-start` command;
one second of device time after the actor spawned, `CGamePersistent::OnFrame`
begins the fixture, polls it every frame, and quits after writing the CSV.

**Compare.** `tools/compare-physics-capture.py <server.csv> <client.csv>`:
identical object, structure, fixed step, gravity and drop; rest and lifted
positions within 0.01 m (H06); the same floor material; per-step positions
within 0.01 m and velocities within 0.01 m/s over the common free-fall steps,
positions within 0.05 m after contact; first-contact steps within 2; both
settled, undestroyed, final positions within 0.05 m, both end rows `ok=1`.

## Findings

- **Gravity is 19.62 m/s² and velocity is divided by 1.01 every step.** Both
  are native defaults (`Physics.cpp:31, 54`), so a 1 m drop takes 34 steps
  (0.34 s) and lands at 5.5 m/s rather than the 4.4 m/s of real gravity. Any
  future server-side prediction or comparison must use the engine's recurrence,
  not textbook free fall.
- **Sleeping is phase-dependent.** The step at which the world deactivates the
  settled body varied from 54 to 73 across four runs of the same drop, because
  the disabling check runs on a world-wide counter; it is not a determinism
  defect of the server. The compare tool reports it and asserts the rest state.
- **A 1 m drop of a 100 kg crate does not break it.** The destroyable object's
  collision-damage receiver sent no hit (the object survived, ok=1 on both sides).

## Limits

- **One body, one drop, one level.** A single wooden crate on `k00_marsh`,
  lifted 1 m with zero velocity and no rotation. No sliding, stacking, joints,
  ragdolls, character capsules or object-to-object collisions were exercised
  (S02-T04c, S02-T06).
- **Same code on both sides.** As in S02-T03c/d, the client fixture runs the
  same CPU code on the same data; this shows the renderer-free process steps
  physics the same way, not that the physics is independently correct.
- **No bullets or hits (H07)** and no AI reaction to the falling body (H04/H08).
- **The server's ticks are real-time paced** by the smoke driver (one 20 ms
  tick per 20 ms of wall time), so the fixture's wall time equals its simulated
  time; faster-than-real-time stepping was not tested.
- **Client window.** The client capture opens an 800×600 game window for about
  70 s.
