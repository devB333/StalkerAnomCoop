## What was defined

- **Transition id** `(authority_epoch << 32) | serial`: never repeated in an
  incarnation, never continued across a restart (contract §1).
- **State machine** Idle → Proposed (30 s) → Agreed | Refused | Cancelled |
  Expired | Aborted (requester/partner left or died, combat, changer gone)
  → Idle; one transition at a time (§2, §5). The pure state machine is
  `coop::proto::TransitionCoordinator`; the engine host wraps it.
- **Request** (kind 11, target the level changer): checks in order — stored
  outcome, session and living actor, an enabled `CLevelChanger` online, the
  actor inside its shape (the engine's `CCF_Shape::Contact`) or within 1 m
  of its sphere, the partner in the world with a living actor, no NPC
  hunting either actor; then the proposal to both players (§3).
- **Answer** (kind 12: `u64 id, u8 accept/refuse/cancel`): refused unless
  the id is the live proposal's (28) and the answer comes from the right
  party (29); an accept also needs the partner alive and the party out of
  combat (§4).
- **Disconnect policy:** a party member's disconnect aborts the proposal;
  a rejoin starts over; no one-player consent (§5, R10). A request while
  the partner is away or dead is refused (26) — decision T01-3 for the user.
- **No load:** an agreed transition ends with `travel-agreed … executor=none`;
  S09-T02 takes it from there.
- **Client:** touching a changer prompts (USE requests, DROP declines); the
  partner's prompt (USE/DROP); the requester can cancel; every end shown.
  The native change-level path stays refused (L14).

## Limits found here

- The level changer prompt and consent were driven by the step driver and,
  in tv-4, by a few hands-on key presses; a full hands-on session by the user
  (two windows, the prompts, USE/DROP) is still to be done.
- Transitions are not durable before Agreed (contract §8); S09-T02 adds the
  record at the barrier.
- "In combat" is the NPCs' chosen-enemy state only (contract §8).
- A request while the partner is away is refused: decision T01-3 for the
  user at G09 (one-player travel would need approval, R10).
