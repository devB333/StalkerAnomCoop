## What was built

**The controller's attacks target either player (T02c-1).** With two
participants `Actor()` refuses on the server, so S03-T04 turned the tube
attack off (`CControllerPsyHit::check_start_conditions`,
`check_conditions_final`). The attack now picks the controller's enemy when
it is a participant actor (`target_actor`; single player and a client keep
the one actor) and holds it for the whole attack (`m_target_id`). The
gameplay stays on the server: the psy hit (`Hit_Psy`, `tube_damage`), the
impulse, the weapon block event. The viewer's effects go to that player's
client as the presentation `ControllerPsyHit` (object: the controller;
flags: the phase): 1 the prepare sound, 2 the glide (the target point and
the glide time: the client adds `CControllerPsyHitCamEffector` from its own
camera, hides the HUD, plays the start and pull sounds and the fire
particles), 3 the hit (the hit sounds, particles), 4 the stop (the effector
removed, the HUD back), 5 and 6 the sound states, 7 the control hit
(`CController::control_hit`: its camera and post-process effectors from the
replica's `control_effector()`). The controller replica on the client plays
its own sounds. Server log `psy-hit controller= target= slot= phase=
health=`; client log `psy-hit-applied controller= phase= replica=`.

**Auras are each viewer's own (T02c-2).** `monster_aura` (psy, fire,
radiation, base) is presentation only: a post-process and a looped 2D
sound scaled by distance. On a client `Actor()` is its own actor, but a
creature there is a remote replica whose scheduled update (where
`CBaseMonster` runs its auras) does not run (psy-1), so on a co-op client
the client link updates every presented creature's auras from its own
frame, five times a second (`present_actors` →
`CBaseMonster::replica_update_auras`, `m_aura_cl_time`; psy-2 showed the
replica's `UpdateCL` does not run either) and the scheduled update skips
them; the server computes
none. The client's engine log notes `aura-pp-add` / `aura-pp-remove`.

**Fixture (`--psy c1,c2`, profile `psy_party`, server-driven;
`psy_fixtures`).** Player 1 is moved about 120 m from player 2 (player 1
only, L58); a controller (`m_controller_normal`, else `m_controller_e`) is
spawned 15 m in front of player 2; the fixture waits up to 90 s for the
attack to hit, lets the stop phase reach the client, then kills the
controller and reports the hits per player. `compare-coop-psy.py` checks
the six points in its header.

## Limits found here

- The controller's camera steering during the glide and its possession
  steering are not presented (T02c-3).
- The weapon block event runs on the server's actor; the client's own
  weapon follows through the server's weapon state.
- The psy-dog aura, the burer, the bloodsucker and the poltergeist are
  S10-T02d/e.
