## What was built

**The door (T04c-2).** A client's USE on a script-usable world object (a
door, a switch; not an item, a living thing or a level changer, which keep
their own kinds) is the use-object intent (kind 17): `take_use_object`
checks the reach (3.5 m) and runs the object's use callback on the server
in the player's context (`CUsableScriptObject::use` enters it), so the
installed `ph_door` logic (`esc_trader_door.ltx`: `on_use` with its faction
check) decides. A scripted joint drive
(`cphysics_joint_scripted::SetForceAndVelocity`, the door's open and close)
is presented to every client (`Presentation::JointDrive`: bone id, force,
velocity, axis), which applies it to the same joint of its own copy, so the
door swings on both screens. The client's own copy does nothing on its own
(before: the local door opened while the server's stayed shut, and the
server's correction was "an invisible wall", the user's demo-3…5).

**The purse (T04c-4).** The per-player survival event (12) carries the
player's money; the client applies it to its actor without an event, so the
HUD and the trade window read the server's purse (the replica policy drops
`GE_MONEY`).

**Trade (T04c-1, T04c-5).** The talk window's Trade button is live in a
co-op talk and sends the trade-open intent (18) for the NPC the player
talks to. The server starts the engine's trade session for this buyer in
his context (`CTrade::StartTradeEx` on the trader: the installed
`trade_manager` sets the trader's buy and sell conditions for him), prices
every item of the stock and of the buyer's inventory with
`CTrade::GetItemPrice` as the window would, and sends the table as
CampaignEvent 15 (in parts of 56). The client opens the native
`CUIActorMenu` trade mode against the replica trader when the last part
arrives; `CTrade::GetItemPrice` on a co-op client reads the table (an item
not in it is unpriced: 0). A buy or sell button sends the items as one
trade intent (19: the bought and the sold refs); nothing moves locally. The
server re-prices them, checks the owners (where the client saw them), the
tradeability and the purse, journals one `trade` record (`kRecordTrade` 14:
the item moves as transfer records plus both purses after) before
`CTrade::TransferItem` applies it in the buyer's context (the engine's own
path: the trader's callbacks, GE_TRADE_SELL/BUY on the server's level),
answers, and sends the table again. The clients see the moves as Reparent
and rebuild the window's lists (`CUIActorMenu::CoopTradeRefresh`); a
refusal shows the window's own message box (not enough money / cannot
trade). Closing the window (20) or the talk closes the trade. On recovery
the record is replayed through the transfer adapter plus `set_money`
(`trade-replayed`).

**Repair (T04c-7).** The mechanic's repair dialogue already runs on the
server in the talking player's context (S10-T04a); its money reaches the
client through the purse. The item's condition reaches the client in the
inventory layout event (kind 9): each entry carries a `condition` byte
(condition × 255) after the amount, both layout hashes include it, and the
client sets the item's condition when the byte differs (`apply_layout`,
logging `layout-condition` for a step larger than two, so a shooter's own
small wear is corrected quietly). The trade fixture wears player 1's first
weapon to 0.35 and repairs it to 1.0 two seconds later the way
`item:set_condition` does (`fixture-trade worn|repaired`).

**An artefact on the belt (L48).** The trade fixture gives player 1 the
first installed outfit section with `artefact_count > 0` and an
`af_medusa`, wears the outfit through `take_equip` (the outfit slot) and
puts the artefact on the belt (`fixture-trade belt-outfit|belt`); the
client's layout puts it on its belt too (`layout-belt`).

**The partner's attack animations (T04c-6, the K1 follow-up 2, patch
0066).** With every first-person motion of the other player's item blocked
(K1), its third-person body never swung, reloaded, drew or holstered: the
torso animation is picked from the active weapon's `GetState()`
(`ActorAnimation.cpp`), which patch 0065 forces to idle on a replica. The
server's sampled `weapon_state` (snapshot kWeapon, `apply_actor_outcomes`)
is stored on the replica actor (`CActor::replica_set_weapon_state`,
`Actor_Movement.cpp`) and read by the animation picker alone
(`replica_anim_weapon_state`, weapons and missiles); the weapon object
stays idle, because eFire on it would fire on the client
(`CWeaponMagazined::UpdateCL`). An attack state is held at least 350 ms,
and a shot counted between samples kicks it too. Logs: the client's
`remote-weapon-state id= state=a->b slot= item=`, the engine log's
`remote-torso id= state= motion=slot/idx`, the stats counter
`weapon_states_presented`. The combat program got a knife leg
(`equip-knife`, `face-sky`, `W-knife`) and `compare-coop-combat.py` the
`partner_anim` check.

## Limits found here

- **The partner's weapon switch** was not shown on the other client (the
  user, demo-1; the G07 K1/K5 hands-on retest was never done). trade-2's
  diagnostics found the cause: `CInventory::Update` deactivates the
  replica's active item through `SendDeactivateItem` → the state event,
  whose hide motion is blocked for another player's item (K1, patch
  0043), so the item sat in `eHiding` and the next weapon never showed.
  **Patch 0065** makes a showing or hiding state of another player's item
  instant presentation (`CHudItem::OnStateSwitch`, `SendHiddenItem`);
  trade-3 shows the switch completing. **Not yet seen by hand.** The
  partner's **melee swing and other attack animations** were not played on
  a replica either (the user: friendly fire and shooting work, "there is
  just no swinging animation"): the K1 motion block covered them; **patch
  0066** presents them from the server's weapon state (T04c-6; anim-1).
  **Not yet seen by hand.** Still not presented: the partner's **zoom
  pose** (`IsZoomed` is false on a replica, so an aiming partner shows the
  hip pose) and the aim yaw/pitch of the arms (separate, S06-T03's).
  The 350 ms attack hold is a heuristic: a gun's single shot shows the
  firing pose for 350 ms, a held burst for its duration.
- **The combat fixture's second actor has no knife** (`arm` gives a pistol
  and ammunition only), so its knife leg is refused (reason 4) and only
  player 1's swings are seen (by client 2); a fixture inventory fact.
- **Repair durability**: a repair's payment and the item's condition are
  durable through the checkpoint only (the mechanic's dialogue is a script
  action, not a journal record); a crash between the repair and the next
  checkpoint reverts both together. The field repair UI (`ui_workshop`)
  stays local and unsupported. The trade fixture sets the condition the
  way the dialogue does; no mechanic dialogue was run.
- **The trade window's filters and stock changes by the other player**:
  the table is resent after each trade of the open buyer only; another
  player's purchase while the window is open is noticed at the next
  request (refused `TradeRefused` if the item is gone).
- **Items with uses or charges** are priced by the engine's `Cost()` as in
  single player; the trader's deficit factor is `#if 0` in this engine.
- **Donate** (the window's donate action) goes through `TransferItem(…,
  free)` locally and is not routed: unsupported in co-op (no run).
- **Amended by S10-T04f (T04f-6):** the trade window a client opens is
  Anomaly's installed Lua window (`ui_inventory.script`), not
  `CUIActorMenu`: the engine window's hooks above (`UIActorMenuTrade.cpp`)
  never run in Anomaly; patch 0067 routes the Lua window's `transfer_item`
  and `give_money` through the trade intent instead. trade-3/4 proved the
  server's trade with the server-driven fixture; the window path is the
  user's pending hands-on check.
- A `sound-volume` presentation flood (6 per tick per client for sounds
  the clients lack) was seen in every demo since S06-T08; not caused here,
  not fixed here.
