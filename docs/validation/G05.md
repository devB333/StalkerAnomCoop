## What is not covered (carried)

- **Not a playable campaign.** Movement is the only player action that
  reaches the server; no fire, use, jump, inventory, trade or dialogue
  (S06-T04, S08). No client prediction: own movement waits for the server
  (S06-T03). Movement is native physics on the server but not validated
  against cheating (S06-T01).
- NPCs and items do not move on clients after spawning (S06-T02).
- No gameplay scripts on clients (HUD scripts, news, PDA updates) until the
  presentation split (S08/S10). Player 2's client shows a local
  "Mission complete" news item and a `CRandomTask` error from an unknown
  source; it changes nothing shared.
- Player 2's name, faction and look are the engine's defaults for a new
  actor.
- One Windows host, loopback, sessions under 5 minutes, one level
  (`k00_marsh`). No LAN or internet (S11-T01); the active man-in-the-middle
  limit (G04) is unchanged.
- Open defects: the client ALife scheduler stall after load (S05-T02, not
  needed by a replica); rare client access violation at exit (2 of ~40 S05-T03
  runs, none in S05-T04); the GameState smoke's intermittent activity-fixture
  walk stop (smoke-143, 148, 151).
- The G01 waivers and the G02–G04 carried limits are unchanged.
