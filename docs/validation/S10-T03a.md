## What the engine and the scripts do (source)

- **Online/offline switching** already uses both players (S03-T04, patch
  0024): `CALifeSwitchManager::refresh_activation_anchors` takes the alive
  participant actors' server entities each ALife update and
  `activation_distance` is the distance to the nearest
  (`alife_switch_manager.cpp:61-90`); every switch site calls it. A detached
  participant keeps its slot and its actor, so its actor stays an anchor.
  S03-T04 proved the rule headless (separation, disconnect, return); this
  task checks it with real clients and adds the identity checks.
- **Respawn is the installed scripts'**: `se_smart_terrain:update`
  (`smart_terrain.script:1238-1285`) sets `self.dist_to_actor` from
  `alife():actor()`, the ALife actor (actor 0, player 1), then calls
  `try_respawn` (`:1597-1720`), which returns while `dist_to_actor <
  respawn_radius` (150 m by default) and otherwise, after `respawn_idle`
  game seconds, creates a squad from `respawn_params` through
  `SIMBOARD:create_squad`. A squad's `dist_to_actor`
  (`sim_squad_scripted.script:228-229`, read by task markers in
  `task_functor.script` and the survival help rule) is also from actor 0.
  **So a squad could respawn in front of player 2** whenever player 1 was
  150 m or more away. Nothing in the engine redirects `alife():actor()`
  (`alife_simulator_script.cpp:712`).

## What was built

- `campaign.nearest_participant_distance(x, y, z)`
  (`CampaignScriptContext.cpp`): the distance to the nearest alive
  participant actor's server entity, the same anchors as the switch; −1 on a
  client or in single player.
- Compat-layer wrappers (server only, `S10-T03a compatibility layer ALife
  wrap: respawn+squad` in the engine log): `se_smart_terrain.try_respawn`
  sets `dist_to_actor` from the nearest player before the installed code
  reads it (counter `respawn_guarded`); `sim_squad_scripted.check_invulnerability`,
  called right after the squad's `dist_to_actor` is set, does the same for
  squads (`squad_dist`). Decision T03a-1.
- **Fixture** `--alife c1,c2` (profile `alife_party`, server-driven,
  `CoopServerHost.cpp` `alife_fixtures`): a baseline identity scan of the
  whole ALife registry (a member in two squads, a member whose group is not
  its squad, two alive creatures with one name, two objects with one story id;
  the baseline's dead kept for a resurrection check) and the server's CPU with
  the players together; player 1 moves to a smart terrain 300+ m from player
  2 (player 1 only, L58); CPU apart; **guard**: the respawn smart terrain
  beside player 2 is forced to respawn (its timer and counts cleared,
  `try_respawn` called) and must create nothing; **respawn**: the respawn
  point nearest player 2 clear of both players' radius is forced and must
  create one squad; a non-story stalker near player 2 is killed; the scan
  repeats; the subjects go into the scripts' saved state
  (`alife_storage_manager` `coop_alife_fixture`) and a checkpoint is asked
  for; while client 2 is away the online creatures around its actor are
  counted. After a server restart (the marker found) or a travel (another
  level), the victim must still be dead under its name, the respawned squad
  must exist once with its members, no other squad from its respawn point
  may have appeared, the scan must be clean; after a restart the guard is
  checked again. `tools/compare-coop-alife.py` (variants `restart`, `travel`)
  checks the lines and every client's final replica against the server
  (`compare-coop-world.py`).
- **Patch 0076** `alife-respawn` (SHA-256
  `9a7bd0db72b9baa6d87c1ab94a9a3ba71b550eabd1aef8715ef40c5ae9ca4c9c`;
  `CampaignScriptContext.cpp`, `CoopConfig.{h,cpp}`, `CoopServerHost.cpp`):
  the series of 76 patches reconstructs engine-work byte for byte in
  `builds/stage2/patch-check-88b` (`builds/logs/patch-reconstruction-88.json`).
- **Tool fix:** `compare-coop-world.py` removed an entity from the server's
  online set on any lifecycle row after its spawn, including a `reparent`
  (an item dropped or taken, S08); only a `despawn` does now, and a row cut
  short by a killed server is skipped, and a second spawn of actor 0 (a
  travel's reload) starts a new set. Earlier reports that used it on runs
  without reparents or travel are unaffected.

## Limits found here

- Player 1 only is moved by the fixture (L58); player 2's long moves stay
  untested.
- `sim_squad_scripted:add_squad_member` marks a newly spawned member's
  vertex for its online spawn only when it is within the switch distance of
  actor 0 (`db.spawned_vertex_by_id`, `sim_squad_scripted.script:619-622`);
  near player 2 alone such a member spawns at its smart terrain's position
  instead of its own vertex. Not seen to matter (on most levels every point
  is within 750 m of player 1).
- L53 (the world runs around an absent player's actor) still holds and is
  now a decision (T03a-2).
