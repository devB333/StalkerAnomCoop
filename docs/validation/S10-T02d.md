## What was built

The S03-T04 table listed these abilities as off with two participants
(`Actor()` refuses on the server). Each now follows the S10-T02c pattern:
the target is the monster's participant enemy, the gameplay stays on the
server, the viewer's effects go to the target's client.

**The vampire grab (T02d-1).** `CStateBloodsuckerVampireExecute` accepts
either participant as its enemy, holds it for the grab (`m_target_id`),
blocks its weapons and locks its inventory on the server, and presents
`MonsterEffect` to that player's client: 1 the grab (the HUD off, the
inventory locked), 2 the camera (`CAI_Bloodsucker::ActivateVampireEffector`:
the bloodsucker's head position and the time; the client adds
`CVampireCameraEffector` from its own head and `CVampirePPEffector` from the
replica's `pp_vampire_effector`), 3 the release. The damage
(`execute_vampire_hit`) was the enemy's already.

**The anti-aim and the burer (T02d-1).** `anti_aim_ability` watches the
monster's participant enemy: on the server the player's aim is its input's
torso (`replica_torso`: the camera's world yaw and pitch); the camera kick is
the existing `CameraEffector` presentation to that player (the `.anm` from
the ability's effector section) and removed with `CameraEffectorRemove`. The
burer's stamina hit and weapon drop (`CBurer::StaminaHit`, the anti-aim's
callback) land on the same player, its gravi-prepare particles stop on the
enemy they started on, and its shield reacts to either player reloading
(`actor_is_reloading_weapon(enemy)`).

**The poltergeist (T02d-2).** The detection (`CPoltergeist::update_detection`)
gates the telekinesis and the fly-around, so on the server it runs against
the nearest participant (`detection_actor`); the telekinesis targets that
player (`CPolterTele::m_target`, every throw). The detection post-process
and the actor visibility are each viewer's own: on a co-op client the
replica computes them for its own actor (`replica_update_auras`, now virtual,
driven by the client link as in S10-T02c).

Server log `monster-ability monster= target= slot= ability= value=` (abilities
`vampire-grab`, `vampire-release`, `anti-aim`, `stamina`, `tele`); client log
`monster-effect-applied monster= phase=` and the presentation lines.

**Fixture (`--mutant c1,c2`, profile `mutant_party`, server-driven;
`mutant_fixtures`).** Player 1 is moved about 120 m away (player 1 only,
L58); three stages run in turn near player 2, each up to 60 s and ended by
its ability's first event, with player 2's health held at 1: a
`bloodsucker_normal` 5 m away with its vampire wish forced
(`coop_force_vampire`), a `burer_normal` 10 m away with player 2's pistol
drawn and the anti-aim forced each tick (`set_force_anti_aim`), an
`m_poltergeist_normal_tele` 8 m away with its detection held full
(`coop_force_detection`). Each monster is killed when its stage ends.
`compare-coop-mutant.py` checks the six points in its header.
