# S06-T04 — One authoritative weapon/combat slice

**Status: Completed (2026-10-21).** Engine `c57fe5b9` + patches 0001–0034;
protocol v1.4 (`proto-build-30`). Design:
[co-op session](../coop-session.md#authoritative-weapon-slice-s06-t04).

## What changed

**Protocol v1.4** (workspace files `messages.h/.cpp`, `snapshot.h/.cpp`,
tests):
- Snapshot component `kWeapon` (bit 7): active slot (≤ 15), weapon state,
  rounds in the magazine, shot counter; 6 bytes. Deltas and merges as for the
  other components.
- Input button `kButtonFire` (bit 1); bit 0 is walk (`kButtonWalk`).
- `ActionKind`: 1 activate slot, 2 reload.

**Engine (patch 0034)**:
- **Fire is an input** (`CoopServerHost.cpp`). On a change of the fire bit
  the server presses or releases the native `kWPN_FIRE` on the player's own
  actor (`actor_input_press/release`, `Scope::Self`). The weapon decides
  whether, when and how it fires; bullets fly in the server's bullet manager
  and hit at the server's present time. A silent or detached player's trigger
  is released.
- **Fire ray per actor** (`Actor_Weapon.cpp`). `CActor::g_fireParams` took
  every actor's ray from the HUD pick, the view player's camera. An actor
  other than the view one now fires along its own camera (`Cameras()`), which
  `cam_Update` keeps for every actor. Single-player has one actor and keeps
  the HUD pick.
- **Actions.** Kind 1 activates a slot (0 holsters, 1–6 must hold an item);
  kind 2 presses the native reload key. Reasons: 1 unsupported kind,
  2 malformed params, 3 no live actor, 4 nothing to act on. Other kinds, a
  forged hit included, are refused with reason 1.
- **Outcomes.** Each actor's snapshot carries `kHealth` and `kWeapon`. The
  host counts shots as the magazine's decreases. A client applies health,
  slot and rounds when the server's value changes and shows the other
  player's shots from the counter (`CWeaponMagazined::replica_present_shot`:
  sound, flame, smoke, no bullet). When the server puts an item in a slot
  (`CInventory::Slot` sends no event), the client moves the same kind of item
  from the backpack into it.
- **Client weapon state.** The replica refused every game event from its own
  level, so its own weapon could not change state (`CHudItem::SwitchState`
  waits for the `GE_WPN_STATE_CHANGE` echo). That event is now let through
  (`CoopReplica.cpp`, counted as `weapon_states_allowed`). Hits stay refused:
  combat-4's clients refused 5 local `GE_HIT` events each.
- **Hits between participants** (`Actor.cpp`, `CActor::Hit`). The installed
  pre-hit callback `_G.CActor__BeforeHitCallback` (`_g.script:1221`) drops a
  hit when its source is a stalker or actor other than id 0 and friendly to
  `db.actor` (`IsStalker` includes actors). The first participant's actor
  (id 0, `db.actor`) could hurt the second; the second could hurt nobody,
  itself included (combat-2 and combat-3: none of client 2's 10 aimed shots per run hurt actor 0). Hits
  between two participant actors now skip that callback and follow one co-op
  rule both ways: a player's own hits apply; another player's apply when the
  profile allows friendly fire (`friendly_fire`, default 1). Other hits on an
  actor still go through the callback.
- **Fixtures** (`CoopConfig`; `prepare-coop-session.py --combat`): `arm`
  gives each participant actor a PM (into slot 2, not drawn) and two boxes of
  9x18 FMJ; `combat_heal` restores an actor below 40 % health to 100 % (logged
  as `heal`). Items are spawned the way the script's `alife():create` does
  for an online parent (spawn packet through `xrServer::Process_spawn`).
- **Test driver** (`CoopClientLink.cpp`, profile `script = 3`) and records:
  server `coop-combat.csv` (every participant actor at each snapshot round:
  health, slot, weapon state, rounds, shots, last hitter, position), client
  `coop-combat-client.csv` (what the client shows of both actors, 10 Hz), a
  `fire-press` line with the fire ray against the other participant.
- **Tools:** `tools/compare-coop-combat.py` (new), `prepare-coop-session.py
  --combat`; `Build-Stage2.ps1` and `prepare-coop-session.py` default to
  `proto-build-30`.

## The combat program

Each player in turn (the second starts 45 s after the first), then both:

| Step | What the client does | Check |
|---|---|---|
| holster, equip | ActionIntent slot 0, then slot 2 | both accepted; the server's actor holds nothing, then slot 2, each within 3 s |
| W-aim-fire | aims at the other actor's chest every frame; a press every 300 ms for 1.5 s | ≥ 1 shot and ≥ 1 hit on the other actor |
| reload (×4) | ActionIntent reload | accepted; the magazine fuller within 5 s |
| W-hold | the key held 1 s at the sky | ≤ 1 shot (the PM is semi-automatic) |
| W-rapid | a press every 80 ms for 1 s | 2–12 shots |
| W-dup | as W-rapid, every input batch sent four times | no more shots than W-rapid + 1 |
| W-empty | a press every 100 ms for 3 s | the magazine reaches 0; no shot after that |
| W-move-fire | strafes while aiming at the other actor's chest and pressing every 300 ms | moved ≥ 0.5 m and ≥ 2 shots |
| W-forged | ActionIntent kind 7 "hit 1.0 health=0" at the other actor | refused; no hit, no shot |
| W-simultaneous | both re-equip (205 s) and reload (207 s), then at server time 215 s fire at each other for 1.2 s | first shots within 400 ms |
| agreement | — | each client's health, slot and rounds for both actors are values the server held in the 600 ms before the row (own rounds also up to 400 ms after) in ≥ 95 % of rows, and equal at the end |

A hit is a fall of at least 1 % health between two server rows; bleeding from
earlier hits falls slower.

**Rule changes after seeing data** (all before the final runs):
- *Reload window 3 s → 5 s* (combat-1): the PM's reload took 3.1 s from the
  intent to a full magazine.
- *Forged check*: combat-1 counted bleeding as damage (70 units in the
  forged leg). It now counts hits (≥ 1 %) and the shooter's shots.
- *Agreement*: combat-2 gave 91–94 % against the server's value 150 ms
  earlier. The misses were timing: the shooter's own rounds lead the server
  (local presentation), health arrives sooner than 150 ms, and rows before the
  first outcome had nothing to show. It is now membership in the server's
  values over the 600 ms before (400 ms after for the own rounds), rows before
  the first outcome skipped, and the active slot added.
- *Equip*: in combat-4 slot 2 was already active (the fixture's `Slot` drew
  it), so the intent changed nothing. The fixture now slots without drawing,
  slot 0 holsters, and the program holsters before it equips.
- *Simultaneous time 170 s → 215 s*: in combat-5 the second client reached
  its program end at server time 194 s (slower load).
- *Aim*: in combat-8 (5 % loss, 40 ms lag) client 2's five aimed shots all
  missed. The driver aimed level at eye height, the target's head, and the
  weapon's recoil lifted each later shot (`fire-press` pitch 0 → 0.118). The
  driver now aims at the chest (1.2 m) from its eye and re-aims every frame of
  an aimed leg (W-aim-fire, W-move-fire, W-simultaneous), as a player
  correcting recoil would. combat-7/8 are kept as earlier runs.
- *Re-equip before the simultaneous leg*: in combat-10 client 1 held nothing
  at 215 s. During its W-move-fire leg Fanat (`esc_2_12_stalker_fanat`, id
  19409) hit actor 0 (health 1.000 → 0.862, last hitter 19409), and in the
  same snapshot round actor 0's active slot went to 0. Both clients showed
  that slot (agreement passed). Which installed script put the weapon away was
  not traced. Both players now send activate slot 2 at server time 205 s and
  reload at 207 s.

What this shows, against the acceptance criteria:
- **Clients cannot report damage, hits or rewards as truth.** No client
  message carries one: fire is a key state and actions are kind 1/2 only. The
  forged-hit intent was refused (reason 1) with no shot and no hit. Each
  client's own bullets hit things locally, and the replica refused those hits
  (`GE_HIT` 13–15 per client), so the server's health is the only one.
- **Server ammo and health outcomes agree on both clients.** 99.4–99.7 % of
  10 Hz rows show a value the server held in the 600 ms before (the own rounds
  up to 400 ms after), and the last rows equal the server's for both actors,
  nominal and impaired.
- **Fire cadence and input timing are the weapon's.** Holding the key fires
  once (semi-automatic); 12 presses a second give 6 shots; sending every batch
  four times changes nothing (6 = 6); an empty magazine stops firing until a
  reload.
- **Simultaneous shots** resolve in one server tick, both sides hit.

## Limits

- One weapon (PM, semi-automatic, 9x18 FMJ), one target type (the other
  player's actor), no NPC target (S06-T05) and no death (the clients keep
  actors alive; `combat_heal` keeps the fixture going).
- Hits are resolved at the server's present time with no lag compensation: a
  moving target must be led by the round trip plus the 100 ms render delay.
- The shooter's own weapon fires locally for presentation (sound, flash,
  recoil); a local shot the server does not fire (for example after the
  server's magazine ran out first) is only corrected in the round count. Reload
  and equip are not predicted; the client waits for the server.
- The aim sent to the server is the client's camera, including the local
  recoil the client's weapon added.
- Hits between participants skip the installed `actor_on_before_hit`
  callbacks, so mods listening there do not see them. Friendly fire is on by
  default; the policy is the user's call at G06.
- Fanat (the companion NPC at the fixture's base) can strike an actor and its
  weapon is then put away (combat-10); the installed script responsible was
  not traced.
- The client's slot fix moves "the same kind of item" from the backpack, so
  two different items with the same slot could be confused. Inventory
  replication is S08's.
- One host, loopback, simulated impairment only (L16).
