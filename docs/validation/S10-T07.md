  join (the client runs no squad scripts); its orders go to the server for
  the player's own companions.
- Player 2's "gas mask on" for the rain on the helmet is "a helmet in the
  helmet slot" (`actor_effects` follows player 1's).
- The random ambient one-shots (birds, distant screams) are still placed
  around player 1 and heard by both at their 3D position.
- The music's ambient fade (`snd_volume_music`) is the server's console
  value and does not reach the clients.
- B46: the server does not read the players' "allow fast travel in combat"
  option (`gameplay/fast_travel/on_combat`, `gameplay/backpack_travel/on_combat`):
  a fast travel or a backpack jump is always refused in combat. Its combat
  test is the NPCs' enemy memory (stalkers and monsters), not the installed
  scripts' `fighting_with_actor_npcs` (stalkers only).
- The travel fixture's variant 0 (nine consent rounds) cannot run since
  S09-T02 (round 0's agreement travels); variants 2 and 5 cover execution.
- B47: any installed script window opened on the server is skipped (no
  dialog holder), and every device pause there is refused; a script that
  waited for its window's answer does not get one on the server.
- The travel fixture does not shoot after a travel; `updates` checks the
  per-frame update that a shot's fire point depends on.
- B48: a script can no longer switch a player's torch on the server
  (cutscenes or story effects that force the actor's torch); only the player
  and an empty battery do.
- B53: after participant 1's death its corpse (object 0, the server's script
  actor) travels with the party and lies beside the arrival point; a
  respawned actor's engine base PDA (`device_pda`) stays on the old level.
- B55: the partner's handheld flashlight is a light from its left hand along
  its body's facing (not its aim); the flashlight model in its hand is not
  shown.
- B51: after a long frame (a 70-90 ms hitch) the own actor gets a few
  opposite corrections of 15-25 cm (a record taken in the hitch's catch-up
