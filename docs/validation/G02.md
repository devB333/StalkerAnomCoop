## What is not covered

- **Task (quest) coverage** is one fetch task from one giver on one level,
  with the item placed by the fixture and the dialogue window not shown
  (its phrase functions are called). Storyline, timed, failed and cancelled
  tasks, tasks from simulation NPCs and multi-stage tasks are not observed.
- **B21 — monster AI bound to the controlled entity** ([boundary](../headless-service-boundary.md)):
  the psy dog's aura is guarded (patch 0020); the psi-storm phantom and the
  legacy trader's look-at still dereference `Level().CurrentEntity()` and
  would fault headless if reached. Their per-participant form is Stage 3 work.
- **One level, one save.** All evidence is from `k00_marsh` and the fixture
  save; no level change, no second level and no new game.
- **Short runs.** The longest server run is about two minutes of simulation;
  nothing about long-run stability, memory or drift is observed.
- **No crash or journal guarantee** for the checkpoint (plan.md S02-T06 risk;
  Stage 7).
- **Timing differs from the client** in every behavioural comparison; the
  asserted rules and milestone ordering match, totals do not.
- **Per-run variation**: smoke-81 failed its hearing stage (`npc_walk_events=0`,
  every other run 5–38) and was rerun; the fixtures are observational and
  the simulation is not deterministic across runs.
- **Client quit fault**: capture-10's normal client exited with
  `0xC0000005` after its complete teardown log (the capture itself was
  written); capture-11 from the final source exited 0. Recorded, not
  explained.
