## What was built

**Party info portions (T01-4, T04b-1).** Each actor's known portions live
in the ALife registry (`CInfoPortionRegistry`, keyed by the actor's id, in
every checkpoint). On the campaign server a portion given to or taken from
one participant actor by any path (dialogue, a script inside or outside a
context, the engine) is mirrored to the other participant actors
(`CActor::OnReceiveInfo` / `OnDisableInfo` → `coop_server_info_changed` →
`TransferInfo` on the others, with a recursion guard), so story knowledge
is the party's and `has_info`, the dialogue preconditions and
`has_alife_info` answer the same for both players. The personal portions
(`ui_*`, `*tutorial*`, the sleep flags, `start_equippment_handled`) stay
per actor. A respawned body already received the old body's portions
(S08-T09); mirrored portions reach it too.

**`has_alife_info` means the context's player (T04b-2).** The installed
`has_alife_info(id)` is `alife():has_info(0, id)`, actor 0 by id; inside a
participant context the read goes to that player's actor
(`campaign::info_actor_id`, counted as `info_redirected`); outside a
context it stays actor 0.

**The script state of an actor without the installed binder (T04b-3).**
The installed scripts keep per-object state in `db.storage[id].pstor`
(`save_var` / `load_var`) and persist it through `alife_storage_manager`
(`m_data.game_object[id].pstor_all`, encoded into the `.scoc` on save and
restored by the object's binder on load). Participant 2's actor and a
respawned body have no binder (S03-T03 attaches the installed actor binder
to the script actor only), so they had no `m_data` entry and no load: their
`pstor` was lost on a restart (L32, L44). The server now attaches an
actor-state entry for every participant actor without a binder once it is
online (`actor_state_tick`, a small Lua chunk next to the party adapter):
`m_data.game_object[id]` exists, so the save copies the `pstor`, and a
saved `pstor_all` is restored into `db.storage[id].pstor`
(`actor-state-attached … restored=N`).

## Limits found here

- The mirror follows the engine's info transfer: a portion the installed
  scripts give through `alife():object(id):give_info_portion` to an
  offline participant actor (an ALife-only path) is not seen by the hook
  (`CActor::OnReceiveInfo` runs for online actors). Both participant actors
  are online while their players are in the world; a detached player's
  actor stays online (the detached-actor policy).
- The personal list is the engine's and the installed scripts' portions as
  read in S10-T01's extraction; a mod-added per-body portion would be
  mirrored. The list is a table in `CoopServerHost.cpp` (`personal_info`).
- The actor-state bridge keeps `pstor` and `pstor_ctime`; the installed
  `actor_binder`'s own saved fields (difficulty, the active slot, the
  achievement timers) remain the script actor's only. The per-actor
  systems the binder drives (status, surge cover, achievements) still run
  for the script actor only: L32's remaining part, S10-T05.
- The `info-summary` of the restarted run counts nothing (the fixture's
  recovered phase only reads); the first run's counters are in its log.
