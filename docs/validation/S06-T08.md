## What decides the visuals (source)

Installed scripts from `scripts.db0` (extracted with `tools/xray-db-extract.py`)
and engine `c57fe5b9`:

- **Weather section and blend.** `level_weathers.script` picks one weather
  section per game hour (`update()` 209-240, `select_weather()` 264-381,
  `level.set_weather(section, now)` at 373 with `now=false` on hourly
  changes). The engine blends the section's hourly descriptors by game time
  alone: `CEnvironment::SelectEnvs` and `lerp` (`Environment.cpp:466-553`)
  pick `Current[0]`/`Current[1]` and weigh them by `fGameTime`. With a
  deferred change the two descriptors can belong to different sections (the
  old one until the next hour). So a client needs the **cycle and the two
  descriptors' identities** (their section and `exec_time`), not the section
  name and a forced cut (which S05-T03 sent: `SetWeather(name, true)`).
- **Weather effects (the emission's sky).** `surge_manager.script` calls
  `level.set_weather_fx("fx_blowout_day|night")` at its first stage (632-647)
  and `psi_storm_manager.script` `fx_psi_storm_*` (316-323).
  `CEnvironment::SetWeatherFX` (`Environment.cpp:354-422`) builds a timed
  descriptor sequence relative to the current two descriptors and the game
  time, with `wfx_time` left; `StartWeatherFXFromTime(name, time)` (424)
  starts the same sequence `time` seconds from its end (what the weather
  manager uses after a load). The effect ends by itself in `lerp` when
  `wfx_time <= 0` (`StopWFX`, 436).
- **Lightning.** `CEffect_Thunderbolt::OnFrame` (`thunderbolt.cpp:266`)
  schedules each bolt from `Random` (period and duration of the blended
  descriptor, entry of the collection, life, direction, distance, tilt, and a
  "second" bolt right after with no clap): random per process, so two clients
  never flashed together. The headless server runs no thunderbolt frame
  (`UpdateCPU`, `Environment.cpp:590` only blends).
- **Rain** (`CEffect_Rain`) follows the blended descriptor's `rain_density`
  on each client; nothing to send beyond the blend.
- **The emission's sounds, camera and post-process.** Driven by
  `surge_manager.script` from `level.add_call` (`main_loop`, 45-66, 1 Hz),
  **outside any participant context**: `sound_object:play(db.actor)` /
  `play_at_pos` (begin, impact, waves, siren, 1126-1160), `xr_sound` looped
  rumble and quake with volume fades, `level.add_cam_effector(earthquake_*.anm,
  2, true)` from stage 80 to 214 (723-860), `level.add_pp_effector("psychic.ppe",
  1, true)` 108-210 with `set_pp_effector_factor(1, f)` every second from the
  actor's cover (914-959), `particles_object("crommcruac\\blowout_wave_blend")`
  waves moved each second (466-480, 987-1068). S03-T05 routed camera and
  post-process calls on the server into per-participant presentation outboxes,
  but outside a context they were dropped (`present_to_context`: NoContext),
  sounds and particles were not routed at all, and no outbox reached a client
  (L05).
- **Who runs the surge on the server:** `db.actor` outside a context is actor
  0 (S03-T05 compatibility rule), so `main_loop` runs and the waves and cover
  checks use participant 1's actor.

## Design (patch 0041)

Three new `CampaignEvent` kinds from the server to every live client
(`CoopServerHost.cpp`, applied in `CoopClientLink.cpp`); the world clock
(kind 1) keeps its payload but clients no longer apply its weather name.

1. **Environment (kind 2)**, every second and on change: `bWFX`, `wfx_time`,
   the cycle name, the current weather name, and for each of `Current[0]`
   and `Current[1]` the weather section it was loaded with and its
   `exec_time`. A client outside an effect sets its cycle (`SetWeather(cycle,
   false)`) and points its `Current[]` at the same two descriptors, so the
   blend weight is the same function of the shared game time. When the
   server is in an effect the client starts the same one with
   `StartWeatherFXFromTime(name, wfx_time)`, and re-aligns by shifting the
   effect's descriptors if its own `wfx_time` drifts more than 0.5 s; when the
   server's effect ended, `StopWFX`.
2. **Thunderbolt (kind 3)**: the server draws each bolt as
   `CEffect_Thunderbolt::Bolt` would (same period/duration rules from the
   blended descriptor, `Random` on the server) and sends the collection
   entry, life, direction, distance, tilt and the "second" flag. A live
   client's effect is `remote_driven` (schedules nothing itself) and plays
   the bolt through the new `BoltRemote` (`thunderbolt.cpp`), relative to
   its own camera as the engine does.
3. **Presentation (kind 4)**: every record of a player's outbox and of the
   new **broadcast outbox** (`kBroadcastSlot`, for script calls made outside
   any context, `present_script`) is sent once to that player, in order, with
   the new record fields (handle, position, value, flags). Routed on the
   server: camera effectors (with cyclic/hud/fov/power/speed), post-process
   add/remove/**factor**, `sound_object` play/play_at_pos/stop/stop_deferred/
   volume/position on an actor or no object (`script_sound.cpp`, each
   `sound_object` gets a handle), `particles_object` play/move/stop
   (`script_particles.cpp`). A client plays them on its own actor, keeps the
   server's sounds and particles by handle, and releases them when the world
   ends. A late join starts at the current record serial (nothing before it
   is replayed), so an effector or looped sound added before the join is
   missed until its next add.

Fixtures (`[coop] storm`, `surge`, `psi_storm` seconds after both players
hold an actor; `prepare-coop-session.py --weather c1,c2 [--psi-storm]`):
a forced `w_storm1`, then `surge_manager.start_surge()` (or the psi-storm)
through the script engine. Client program 5 stands and takes a screenshot
every 40 s. Both sides write a weather CSV every second;
`tools/compare-coop-weather.py` measures the checks listed in its header.

### cweather-1 (campaign-220 / client-122): three defects, fixed

Clients 330 s; the fixtures counted from both actors existing. Found:

- **Backlog replay.** The server queued the new events for a link as soon as
  its checkpoint was sent, while the client still loaded its level (39 s).
  Going live, the client replayed 37 s of environment, bolt and presentation
  events over 2.7 s (its weather effect started 8.8 s after the server's,
  bolt 1 was played 18 s late, 345 records counted undelivered). Fix: kinds
  2-4 go to a link only once its client sends input (`client_in_world`), and
  the presentation cursors start at that moment (`present-start` line).
- **A backward clock step ended the effect.** At 347 s both clients' game
  clock stepped back under the S05-T03 rule (3 s tolerance);
  `CEnvironment::SetGameTime` subtracts `TimeDiff`, which wraps a backward
  step to nearly a day, so `wfx_time` went negative, `lerp` stopped the
  effect and the client restarted it from the post-effect descriptors
  (weight jumped 0.43 to 0.94). Fix: on a backward step with an effect
  playing the client adds a day to `wfx_time` first (`clock-step backwards
  wfx-kept`). The re-alignment threshold is 4 s of effect time, above the
  clock tolerance (it had been 0.5 s and fired on every step).
- **Fixtures before the clients were in the world:** the storm and the
  surge fired 36 s and 6 s before the clients were live, so the storm-only
  blend had no rows to compare. The fixtures now count from both clients
  sending input; the surge comes 70 s after (30 s before it had 30 s of
  storm; `prepare-coop-session.py`).

Everything that did run matched: the weather effect's time left differed by
0.42/0.57 game s (p95), camera and post-process state agreed on 257/257
judged seconds, 22 + 61 sounds applied and none missing, 148/156 bolts
within 50-67 ms (the rest were the backlog), 6 screenshots each.

## Limits (carried to their owners)

- **Actor-relative presentation uses participant 1's actor.** Outside a
  context `db.actor` is actor 0, so the blowout waves, the ambient rain loops
  (`ambient\rain_helm` at participant 1's position), the siren position and
  the cover-dependent psychic factor are computed for participant 1 and shown
  to both; participant 2 sees the waves where participant 1 is. Owner:
  S10-T05 (emission gameplay per participant) and the presentation split.
- **The emission's gameplay runs single-player-style on actor 0**: it killed
  participant 1 in cweather-1 and presented the death camera (`surge_02.anm`,
  broadcast) to both players. Owner: S10-T05.
- **HUD indicators are not shown** (`atm_indik_psi_*` custom statics, news,
  dialogs, game over: L05; 6-7 `unknown` records per run).
- **A late join starts at the current record serial**: an effector or looped
  sound added before the join is missed until its next add (the weather and
  the effect itself are state, so they are right at once). Owner: the
  presentation split before S08.
- **Thunder claps and the bolt's geometry are per client** (the clap volume
  gate and the ray pick run locally; the direction, distance and entry are
  the server's).
- Effect time left may differ by up to the clock-step tolerance (3 game s,
  S05-T03); re-alignment past 4 s.
