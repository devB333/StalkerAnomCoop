what policy, fixture and evidence, and which are excluded (plan.md S10-T01;

## Limits of this task

- The passage list is a string scan of `all.spawn`, not a parse of its net
  packets: 4 destinations were inferred from names; the sweep (S10-T03e) is
  the test. Source levels are inferred from the naming convention.
- Counts of `db.actor` and the other forms are static per-file counts; only
  the S03-T05 runtime audit says which lines run. Several families (sleep,
  companions, trade, factions, stashes, emissions' gameplay) have never run
  on the co-op server at all; the matrix plans their fixtures, it does not
  describe their behaviour.
- The Modded Exes runtime overlay (70 scripts) is inventoried, not audited
  beyond the one site the S03-T05 audit caught.
- The content manifest's level list, not the game graph, was used for the
  level set; the game graph's cross-level edges were not read.
- No decision here is implemented; the children begin after the user's
  review.
