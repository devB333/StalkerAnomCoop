## What the scripts do (source)

- The sleep window is the installed Lua UI, `ui_sleep_dialog.script`:
  `TestAndShow` refuses while `db.actor.bleeding` or `radiation` is above 0
  and outside a shelter or tent; `OnButtonSleep` disables the UI, plays
  `sleep.anm` and `surge_fade.ppe` and gives `actor_is_sleeping`; the camera
  effector's callback `dream_callback` calls **`level.change_game_time(0,
  hours, 0)`**, sets `db.actor.power = 1` and sends `actor_on_sleep(hours)`
  (listeners: the survival modules `actor_status_sleep`,
  `actor_status_thirst`, `arszi_psy`, and `surge_manager`,
  `gamemode_ironman`, `xr_patch`); `dream_callback2` saves "<name> - sleep".
  On a co-op client the time skip would be local (the server owns the clock).
- Passing out: the per-player survival updates the server runs (S08-T09,
  `actor_status_sleep.test_blur` / `actor_status_thirst.test_blur`) call
  `force_sleep`, which calls `ui_sleep_dialog.sleep_forced(hours)` (3–9 h)
  and, for thirst, kills the actor with a 5 % chance. On the server that
  would have **skipped the whole world's time** for one player's collapse
  (L49).

## What was built

- **Protocol** (`messages.h`): `kActionSleepRequest` 24 (u8 hours 1–24),
  `kActionSleepAnswer` 25 (u64 id, u8 answer), `kReasonCannotSleep` 42,
  CampaignEvent 19 `sleep` (u64 id, u8 state, u8 requester, u8 partner, u8
  abort, u8 hours, u32 remaining ms; state 11 = blackout); documented in
  [network protocol](../network-protocol.md).
- **Server** (`CoopServerHost.cpp` "S10-T03c"): a second
  `TransitionCoordinator` (the travel consent's state machine; its
  "destination" is the hours). A request is refused while a travel or sleep
  is up (23; a travel request is refused while a sleep is up), without a
  partner in the world (26), in combat (27), or while the requester bleeds,
  is irradiated or passed out (42). Agreed: `level.change_game_time` once,
  `actor_on_sleep(hours)` once in the requester's context (the world's
  listeners and its own survival values), the three survival modules' own
  handlers in the partner's context, both actors' stamina 1, a checkpoint
  ("sleep"), and event 19 to both. A disconnect, a death or combat aborts a
  live proposal; 30 s expire it. **Passing out**: the compat layer wraps
  `ui_sleep_dialog.sleep_forced` on the server (`S10-T03c compatibility layer
  sleep wrap: forced`) to call `campaign.blackout(hours)`: that player's
  input is held for 8 s (`blackout_until_tick`, beside the talking hold in
  `apply_inputs`), event 19 state 11 goes to its client only, and when it
  wakes its survival modules' `actor_on_sleep(hours)` run in its context
  and its stamina is restored; no time skip.
- **Client** (`CoopClientLink.cpp`): at world-live the installed
  `UISleep.OnButtonSleep` is replaced by one that closes the window and calls
  `campaign.client_sleep(hours)` (→ `coop_client_sleep_request`, kind 24); the
  proposal prompts like the travel's (*"Player N wants to sleep N hours. USE
  to sleep too, DROP to refuse."*, the requester waits and may cancel with
  DROP); an agreed sleep fades the screen (`surge_fade.ppe`, `sleep.anm`) and
  says *"You slept N hours."*; a blackout fades it with `surge_02.anm` and
  *"You passed out."*; refusals are said in plain words. The world's time
  reaches the client by its clock event (S05-T03); a client now logs every
  clock jump over 10 minutes.
- **Fixture** `--sleep c1,c2` (profile `sleep_party`, `sleep_fixtures`): the
  intents the keys and window send, made on the server: both players' sleep
  need set to 5000, player 2's radiation cleared; a stale answer, player 2's
  request for 3 h, player 2's own accept, a travel request while it is up,
  player 1's refusal; then a second request accepted; then player 2 passes
  out (`ui_sleep_dialog.sleep_forced(4)` in its context, as its survival
  update would). `tools/compare-coop-sleep.py` checks five points.
