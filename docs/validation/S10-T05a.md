## What the scripts do (source)

- `surge_manager.script` (installed): `main_loop` (45–66, a 1 Hz
  `AddUniqueCall`) runs `CSurgeManager:update` (529–1078) **outside any
  player's context**, so `db.actor` there is actor 0 (S03-T05). One update
  mixes the world (the start condlist, the time factor, the weather effect,
  the NPC and crow kills, the hide task) with one actor's gameplay:
  - the cover check `GetEvent("current_safe_cover")`, set by
    `actor_status.script` (`actor_on_update`, 446–462, from
    `scan_safe_zone_old`, 180–220) for actor 0's binder only;
  - a telepathic hit every 2 game seconds from 108 to 210 s
    (`hit_power`, 405–464: suit, helmet and drugs);
  - two waves started 250 m ahead of the actor (`start_wave`, 466–480),
    moving toward it; wave 1 hits it, wave 2 kills it
    (`kill_actor_at_pos`, 1290–1311) unless it is in cover, immune
    (`surge_immuned`, `level_environment.is_actor_immune`) or Monolith;
  - its sounds, earthquakes (`earthquake_*.anm`), the `psychic.ppe`
    post-process, the HUD indicators and the death camera
    (`surge_02.anm`).
  The file's own timers (`prev_sec`, `prev_game_sec`,
  `currentPPEfactor`, 37–39) are locals of the file.
- `psi_storm_manager.script`: `update` (249–378) drops a vortex every 8–16 s
  within 150 m of `db.actor` (`vortex`, 381–404); 20 s later it hits NPCs
  near it and, unless the actor's cover flag is set, calls
  `vortex_actor_hit` (418–457): a telepathic hit within 200 m and a shock
  hit within 50 m, scaled by distance, with their post-processes.
- S06-T08 routed these calls' presentation to every player (broadcast,
  outside a context): both saw player 1's waves and psychic factor (L28)
  and player 1's death camera (L29); the gameplay hit and killed actor 0
  only.

## What was built (patch 0082, compat layer and fixture)

- **`campaign.enter_slot(slot)`** (`CampaignScriptContext.cpp`): a
  participant's script context by roster slot; **`campaign.mute(on)`**
  drops broadcast presentation while set; `campaign.slot_in_world(slot)`
  and `campaign.note(text)` (an `emission …` line in the co-op log).
- **The surge** (compat layer, `S.emission_wrap = 'surge'`):
  `CSurgeManager.update` runs the installed update **once for the world**,
  muted, with its cover check answered "in cover", so it hurts nobody and
  shows nothing; then, **for each player with an actor, a pass of the same
  installed update in that player's context**. A pass is a table of its
  own stage flags, waves, hit factor, sounds and effector state, whose
  other fields are the manager's (the schedule) and whose world methods
  (start, end, skip, rockets, the hide task) do nothing. Around a pass the
  file's three timers (`debug.getupvalue` / `setupvalue`) and its HUD
  indicator are swapped to the pass's own, `level.set_weather_fx` does
  nothing (the sky is the world's), and the cover flag is that player's own
  (`scan_safe_zone_old` in the player's context). A pass's waves start
  where they would have started on time (120 s and 168 s). When the world's
  surge ends, each pass stops its sounds, waves, effectors and indicators
  (and kills the exposed player when the fate is `kill_at_end`).
  `CSurgeManager.start` counts surges, so a new one starts new passes.
- **A late join** (T05a-5): a player whose client comes back during the
  surge gets a new pass; one-shot sounds older than 3 s are skipped.
- **The psi-storm** (`'+psi'`): the update runs with the cover check
  answered "not in cover", so every vortex reaches `vortex_actor_hit`,
  which runs the installed hit **for each player not in cover, in that
  player's context** (its own distance, protection and post-process).
  `vortex` drops each vortex around the players in turn: a `db.actor`
  override (`S.actor_override`) picks that player's actor while the vortex
  is made, so its particles and sound still go to everyone. Each hit is
  measured on the next update, when it has landed.
- **Fixture** `--emission c1,c2 [--emission-variant 1]` (profile
  `emission_party`, `emission_variant`; `emission_fixtures` in
  `CoopServerHost.cpp`): variant 0 places player 1 in the nearest emission
  cover (`misc\surge_manager.ltx [list]`, a level vertex inside the zone)
  with player 2 outside and starts the installed surge; variant 1 places
  player 1 350+ m from player 2, with no hostile within 80 m of the spot,
  and starts a psi-storm. `tools/compare-coop-emission.py --variant 0|1`.
