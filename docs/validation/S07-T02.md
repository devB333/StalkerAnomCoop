protocol `proto-build-35` (v1.5; one new action kind, 3 `ledger`, no wire

## Deliverable

- **Store library** (project-owned, no engine dependency):
  `protocol/include/coop/store/{file,journal,checkpoint}.h`,
  `protocol/src/store/{file,journal,checkpoint}.cpp`, target `coop_store`.
  - `file.h`: the Windows primitives the contract relies on. `write_file_durable`
    (write, `FlushFileBuffers`, close), `AppendFile` (opened with
    `FILE_FLAG_WRITE_THROUGH`; every append writes and flushes; a failure
    marks it failed for good), `rename_file` (`MoveFileExW` with
    `MOVEFILE_WRITE_THROUGH`, `MOVEFILE_REPLACE_EXISTING` for the pointer),
    `flush_file`, `truncate_file`, listing, SHA-256 (BCrypt), CRC-32. Every
    primitive asks a **failpoint hook** before and after its system call
    (`write:before`, `append:after-write`, `rename:before`, …); the hook can
    fail the call or `TerminateProcess` the process.
  - `checkpoint.h`: `CheckpointStore::publish` runs contract §5 steps 2–8
    from the temporary files the engine wrote (`coop-tmp-gen-<G>.scop/.scoc`)
    plus the supplemental bytes: read back and hash, check the `.scop`
    header (`u32(-1)` then a version ≥ `ALIFE_VERSION`), flush, rename,
    manifest (text, written to a temporary name and renamed), journal
    rotation (`coop-journal-<G>.log` with G's header), the pointer
    (`coop-checkpoint.current`, replaced atomically: **the commit point**),
    retention (everything older than G−1 deleted). Engine failpoints
    `checkpoint:after-temp/-rename/-manifest/-rotation/-pointer` sit between
    the steps. `recover` runs §7 steps 1–3: pointer, verify G (manifest
    parse, campaign, schemas, every file's size and SHA-256, the `.sup` CRC
    and decode, the journal header), else G−1, read every journal ≥ the
    chosen generation, build the replay list (revision > `R_G`, revision
    order), the highest epoch and revision; `coop-tmp-*` leftovers removed.
    Supplemental record (§5.1) and manifest codecs.
- **Engine integration** (patch 0042, `xrGame/CoopPersistence.{h,cpp}`,
  `CoopServerHost.cpp`): `publish_generation(reason)` at the end of a tick
  takes the native save under the temporary name (`M_SAVE_GAME` through the
  in-process server, synchronous, the S02-T06d path), builds the
  supplemental record from the participant links (slot, slot generation,
  player, actor u16 and `EntityId`, alive, position, name), every ALife
  object's `u16 → EntityId` from the identity registry and `next_entity`,
  the operation index, and publishes. Publications: **every join**
  (`start_resync` streams the published generation's `.scop/.scoc`; the
  blob format and the client's `coop-join-N` copy are unchanged), **every
  `checkpoint_seconds`** (profile key, 0 off) and **a clean stop**. With a
  failed store a join falls back to a plain `coop-join-N` save
  (`resync-fallback`), so a player can still join the transient world.
  The store directory is the server runtime's `$game_saves$`.
- **Identity restore** (`CampaignIdentityRegistry::restore_entities`,
  `restore_participant`): before the level loads, the saved `u16 → EntityId`
  map is installed and consumed by the load's `PerformIDgen(saved id, object)`
  (`alife_storage_manager.cpp:152`), `next_entity` continues, and each saved
  participant's player is bound to its saved slot with its saved slot
  generation. A new hook `xrCoopServer_AfterServerReady` (x_ray.cpp, between
  `campaign-server-ready` and the client stages) assigns each saved actor to
  its participant by its saved u16 (`assign_participant_actor`) after
  checking that the u16 resolves to the saved `EntityId`.

## Limits

- **Publication stalls the tick.** The native save (54–78 ms) and the
  store's flush, rotation and pointer steps run on the simulation thread:
  20–620 ms per publication in rec-1 (`checkpoint-published … publish_ms`),
  the slow ones when the volume was busy. Clients see one late tick; not
  measured against the snapshot budget yet. Owner: S07-T06 report (record),
  S11-T01 (move the store steps off the thread while keeping the take inside
  the tick).
- Power loss is not tested and not claimed (contract §5): process
  termination only.
- Retention keeps G−1 and its journal: a generation refused at recovery is
  never deleted by recovery, but a later publication's retention deletes
  anything older than its G−1 (contract §5 step 8).
- The supplemental record holds every ALife object (26,2xx entries, ~260 KB);
  its CRC is checked at recovery, the manifest's SHA-256 covers it.
