## What the traffic was (audit)

`run-emission-4` (S10-T05a, 9 minutes, one surge, client 2 rejoining)
presented **243,142 records** and its outboxes evicted **14,481** (the
outbox holds 256 per slot). Each client logged 122,000–153,000 records it
had no object for (`absent`):

| Kind | c1 absent | c2 absent | Cause (source) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sound-volume` | 119,228 | 150,462 | The scripts set a loop's volume every update even when unchanged (`sound_ambient.script` `update_wind` 233–236, `xr_sound.set_volume_sound_looped`), and the clients had never got those loops' plays (below) |
| `post-process-remove` | 1,824 | 1,560 | `actor_effects.script` removes effects 2015/2020 every update while they are off (B14) |
| `post-process-factor` | 712 | 553 | The surge sets its effector's factor every second, also when unchanged or not on |

Three causes behind the sound volumes (found with `run-presentation-2`'s
per-handle counts, then the installed scripts extracted from
`scripts.db0` with `tools/xray-db-extract.py`):

1. **The level's ambient loops start before anyone joins.**
   `sound_ambient.script` starts `wind_storm1…3` and `underground_drone`
   (`xr_sound.play_sound_looped`, looped themes in `sound_theme.script`
   774–815) at the first actor update, so no client ever got their play;
   their volumes were sent for the whole session (handles 251–254 in
   presentation-2: 605 of 672 absent records; handle 254 once, `0.300` =
   `underground_drone`'s `30 * 0.01` indoors).
2. **The first fix (a replay at a join) never arrived.** The replay was
   queued in the same tick as the client's presentation start, which moves
   the client's cursor past every record queued before it
   (`CoopServerHost.cpp` `send_presentation`, `present-start`); the
   clients' logs start at the serial after the replay. presentation-1/2's
   `loops` check only counted sounds applied at any time, so it passed.
3. **The surge's looped sounds were shared.** A sound theme holds one
   `sound_object` for everyone. The surge's muted world run (S10-T05a)
   plays `blowout_rumble` first, so player 1's pass (same `AC_ID`) found it
   playing and only set its volume; player 2's pass (`AC_ID` is that
   player's id) restarted the same object for player 2. So client 1 got
   volumes for a rumble it never had (handle 483: 66 absent), and **since
   S10-T05a neither player heard the surge's rumble, wave and earthquake
   loops for their own pass**.

## What was built (patch 0084)

- **Sound volume and position** (`script_sound_inline.h`): a volume equal
  to the last one sent (±0.002), and a volume or position for a sound that
  has stopped on the server, are not sent (`volume_deduped`).
- **Live loops at a join** (`script_sound.cpp`, `CoopServerHost.cpp`): the
  routed looped sounds every player hears are tracked (name, flags,
  position, volume) while they play; a player entering the world gets them
  played again **once its presentation has started** (`loops-joined`).
- **Who got a play** (`script_sound.cpp`, `campaign::script_audience`): a
  play in the surge's muted world run is not routed at all (its volumes and
  stops are not sent); a play in a player's context belongs to that player
  (slot and generation). A stop, volume or position for it from **another
  player's context** is not sent (`follow_dropped`: two passes share a
  theme's one-shot, e.g. `blowout_begin`); one made **outside any context**
  goes to the player who got the play.
- **Each surge pass has its own looped sounds** (compat layer,
  `CampaignScriptContext.cpp`): during a pass, `xr_sound.play_sound_looped`,
  `set_volume_sound_looped` and `stop_sound_looped` for the pass's `AC_ID`
  use the pass's own `sound_object`s at its player (`surge-pass-loop`); the
  world's muted run keeps the theme's. A pass replaced at a rejoin stops its
  loops on the server only. The emission wrap reads `surge+loops+psi`.
- **Effectors** (`level_script.cpp`, B14): the server remembers the
  post-process and camera effector ids it presented as added; a remove for
  one that is not on is not sent (`pp_orphans`, `cam_orphans`), nor a factor
  for one that is not on or unchanged.
- Counters at the stop: `presentation-stop live_loops=… volume_deduped=…
  pp_orphans=… cam_orphans=… sounds_replayed=… follow_dropped=…`.

## Limits found here

- The ambient loops (wind, drone, the rain on the helmet) follow player 1's
  state and are heard by both (**B17**); the dynamic combat music plays for
  both when player 1 fights and only its first part reaches the clients
  (**B18**). Both are on the bug list for S10-T07.
- A one-shot sound shared by two players' passes (a theme's
  `sound_object`) is restarted by the second pass on the server; each
  client keeps its own copy, so both hear it, but the first player's copy
  is not stopped early by a stop from the second pass (by design now).
- The orphans measure counts only logged records: the clients do not log an
  applied record of the chatty kinds (volume, position, particle moves).
