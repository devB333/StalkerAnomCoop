## Proposed new carried limits (rows to add on acceptance)

- **Equip, use and eat stay refused on clients** (slot/belt/ruck moves and
  eating do nothing for a client; activate-slot is the only equip action).
  Owner: S10 (or a Stage 8 follow-up if play needs medkits first).
- **A container created after the last checkpoint is not durable**: after a
  restart its committed contents lie on the ground where it was.
- **Consumption after a pickup is not durable**: a used item comes back with
  its pickup record after a restart (until consumption gets a record).
- **Stacks, nested containers and addons move whole**; no split.
- **One quest adapted**; the talk window does not reach the server yet (the
  fixture sends the quest intents; S10-T04); the partner's PDA does not list
  the party task; the bonus stash's map spot is not replicated; news sent
  while a player is away is not replayed.
- **Quest outcomes wait for a publication** (0.1–0.16 s observed); with a
  failed store they are refused and the server stops.
- **The anchor's death with a party task active** is untested.
