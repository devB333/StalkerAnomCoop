## How single player starts a new game (source)

- The creation screen (`ui_mm_faction_select.script` `OnStartGame`,
  1683–1757) writes the choices (name, faction, start location, money,
  loadout, game modes) to `axr_main.config`, which is
  `ini_file_ex("axr_options.ltx")` (`axr_main.script:15`). That file is under
  `$game_config$` (`_g.script:1597`), **not** the app-data folder. The
  screen then starts `all/single/alife/new`.
- `CALifeUpdateManager::load` → `new_game("all")`: the level is the spawn
  data's actor level, the stub `fake_start`.
- `on_game_load` (`ui_mm_faction_select.script:43`, fired by the actor
  binder, `bind_stalker_ext.script:61`) applies the choices to actor 0:
  community `actor_<faction>`, money, name (`_` → space), the loadout (each
  section once, a weapon's ammo by `new_game_loadouts.ltx`), the story
  setup; it records the start location's `lvid`, `gvid` and position
  (`new_game_start_locations.ltx`).
- `actor_on_first_update` (369–418) calls `ChangeLevel` to the start.
  On a campaign server that `M_CHANGE_LEVEL` was refused (L14); nobody
  moved.

## What was built

- **The server starts a new game** (`x_ray.cpp`, `xrCoopServer_NewGame`):
  when the profile says `new_game = 1` and the campaign has no generation to
  recover, the level starts as `all/single/alife/new` (`SaveName` is `all`).
  A recovered campaign always loads (`recovered_save`), so a restart never
  starts again.
- **The creator's choices** are the server's `axr_options.ltx`
  `[character_creation]`, read by the installed `on_game_load` unchanged.
  `prepare-coop-session.py --new-game <faction>:<start location>
  [--new-game-loadout …] [--new-game-loadout2 …] [--new-game-money N]`
  merges them into the runtime's own `gamedata/configs/axr_options.ltx`
  (newgame-1 wrote them to the app-data folder, where the game does not
  look).
- **The start move** (T03f-1): the script's `ChangeLevel`, whether it comes
  as `M_CHANGE_LEVEL` (`xrServer.cpp`, before the refusal) or through the
  compat layer's `campaign.relocate` in a player's context
  (`coop_server_relocate`), is taken once (`new_game_request`). It is
  proposed and agreed in the travel coordinator in one step
  (`new_game_tick`) and carried out by the S09 executor: the take moves
  actor 0 to the start, the destination generation publishes, and the
  level reloads in process. The party-whole check and the hold do not wait
  for players who are not in the world yet. The arrival accepts a slot-1
  link with no actor, and the server resumes at once
  (`new-game-started`).
- **Player 2** (T03f-3): its actor is created when it joins the start level
  (`give_actor`), copying the creator's trader data (community, rank)
  through the existing `like` parameter, with its own name
  (`m_character_name_str`, which the spawn packet carries) and money
  (`new_game_money_2`). Its loadout `new_game_loadout_2` is given once when
  it is online (`give_new_game_loadout`, the creation screen's rules), or
  the faction's kit when none is set.
- `new-game-actor` logs each player's community, name, money, item count,
  level and position once it is in the world.
- `tools/compare-coop-newgame.py`: checks start, creator, partner and
  clients.

## Limits found here

- Player 2's loadout and money come from the server profile, not a screen
  (T03f-2) until S10-T06.
- The creator's game modes (warfare, azazel, ironman, survival) are applied by
  the installed `on_game_load` for actor 0 only; they were not tested in
  co-op. Warfare's random start (`actor_on_first_update`) takes the
  `random_starting_locations` path, not `ChangeLevel`, and is not handled.
- Player 2 stands where `give_actor` finds a free spot beside player 1,
  which may be on another floor (3.2 m lower at Skadovsk).
