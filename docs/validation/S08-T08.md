## What was missing (source and S08-T02's runs)

- A transfer into a container the recovered world lacked kept the item on the
  ground (`transfer-replay-owner-gone`, S08-T02): nothing recorded the
  container itself.
- Only client actions were journaled. The installed scripts change a
  player's inventory by themselves; the clearest case is the deployable
  backpack (`item_backpack.script` `actor_on_item_use`): the use creates an
  `inv_backpack` box, releases the slot-13 backpack and, one frame later
  (`CreateTimeEvent`), `transfer_item`s the ruck into the box. None of that
  was durable.
- Ammunition stacks: the installed merge (`item_weapon.ammo_aggregation`,
  `item_weapon.script:315-397`) runs from the client UI's
  `ActorMenu_on_item_after_move` after a move into a box; on a replica it
  changed counts locally and tried to release items it does not own. The
  layout carried no ammunition counts, so even reloads left the clients'
  box counts stale. No installed path splits a stack.

## The changes

| Part | Server | Client |
|---|---|---|
| Containers (L38) | before the first committed transfer that touches a box or a body after each publication (a client's transfer or a server-made move into it): `entity` record (definition, position, a body's health 0); replay re-creates an absent one under its EntityId | — |
| Server-made changes | every tick starts by comparing each participant actor's children with the end of the previous tick; changes made in the engine frame are journaled as server-origin `pickup` / `transfer` / `tombstone` (operation 0); the observation is taken again at the end of the tick; ammunition boxes, grenades and bolts are not observed | — |
| Stacks (L40) | an ammunition box moved into an inventory box: once the level holds it there, the installed merge runs in the mover's context; 10 ticks later the box's stacks of that section are read and a `stack` record (counts, released stacks) is committed; counts go to clients (CampaignEvent 10); the replay sets counts and releases, waiting for stacks that a deferred transfer re-creates | the local merge is disabled (`item_weapon.ammo_aggregation[_full]` no-ops); amounts and Reparents whose objects are not on the level yet are retried ≤ 10 s; an item-graph point that arrives while Reparents wait is compared once they are applied |
| Layout amounts | CampaignEvent 9 carries u16 amounts: an eatable's uses or an ammunition box's rounds | applies the rounds |
| Record definitions | a pickup's or transfer's spawn state carries the level object's current ammunition count | — |

**Defects found on the way (all fixed in this patch):**

1. **`CInventory::Eat` returns false after applying a use** when the used-up
   item cannot be deleted (the deployable backpack): S08-T07's `take_use`
   took that for a refusal (21) and committed nothing although the effects
   had happened (ct-1). Past the engine's own checks (which `take_use` makes
   first) the use is applied: it is now always recorded.
2. **A use's released items were dropped from its record** (S08-T07): the
   EntityId of a child the use released was looked up after the release
   and found 0 (ct-2: the stash use recorded `effects=0` though it released
   the slot-13 backpack). The EntityIds are captured before the use.
3. **The merge's result was read too early** (ct-1): the installed merge's
   count change and release land a frame later; the result is read 10 ticks
   after the merge ran.
4. **A client's Reparent into a container that was not on its level yet was
   dropped** (ct-1: 12 of 20 item-graph points mismatched after the stash):
   retried until the objects are there.
5. **A merged stack's count was set before the replay re-created the stack**
   (ct-5, ct-7, ct-8): the stack record now waits for its stacks.

## Carried limits

- **L38** (a container created after the last checkpoint is not durable):
  **closed** — containers are recorded and re-created (ct-6, ct-10…ct-12).
- **L40** (stacks, nested containers, addons move whole): **closed** as
  defined: stacks merge on the server as the installed scripts merge them
  and their counts are durable and replicated; no installed path splits a
  stack (decision T08-5); a backpack is one item; the deployed stash is a
  durable box. Weapon addons stay part of their weapon (engine flags, not
  items) and move with it.
- **L36**: narrowed: server-made changes to the players' inventories are
  durable; trade and the quests not adapted remain.
- **L32**: unchanged.

New limits (for the Stage 8 close):

- A use's next-frame script work (the stash's transfers) is journaled one
  host tick later: a termination in between keeps the ruck in the actor and
  no stash (ct-7/ct-11; consistent, nothing lost).
- The loot of a body nobody touched is not durable (decision T08-7).
- Combat consumables roll back with the fight (decision T08-3).
